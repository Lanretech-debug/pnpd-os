# 05-research — Research Notes

## Purpose

This folder holds research notes — market, technical, and product research. Research notes are non-canonical unless promoted through governance.

No research note is created by this scaffold phase.

## Allowed content

- Market research notes (after authorization)
- Technical research notes
- Product research notes
- Discovery artifacts
- README files describing folder governance

## Forbidden content

- Research notes that claim production or adoption readiness
- Research notes that claim implementation authority
- Research notes that override canonical governance
- Private personal notes
- Automation or generated state

## Privacy and Git tracking

`research`. Research notes are non-canonical unless promoted through governance. Market, technical, and product research remain advisory.

## Authority and automation boundary

No automation is authorized. Owner remains final authority.

Research notes are advisory only. Research must not claim production or adoption readiness. Research notes do not grant implementation authority.

Committed Markdown files are the source of truth. Obsidian is the human-facing editor and navigation interface only.
