# TEMPLATE ONLY — Research Note

This template is an authorship aid, not an authoritative record.

No automation is authorized.

Owner remains final authority.

## Intended use

Use this template when recording research notes under `memory/05-research/`.

## Required fields

- `type`: `research-note`
- `project`: `pnpd-os` or project slug
- `tags`: research category tags (e.g. `market`, `technical`, `product`)
- `privacy`: `research`
- `git_tracking`: `committed` or `gitignored`

## Content body

```markdown
# Research — <topic-slug>

## Category

## Source

## Key findings

## Relevance to PNPD

## Open questions

## Related research
```

## Authority boundary

- Research notes are non-canonical unless promoted through governance.
- Research is advisory only.
- Research must not claim production or adoption readiness.
- Research notes do not grant implementation authority.

## Promotion or validation requirements

- Governance review before promotion to canonical status.
- Market, technical, and product research remain separate categories.
