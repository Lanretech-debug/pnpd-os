## Problem Classification

- [ ] **Workflow gap** — A missing step or broken flow in daily operations.
- [ ] **Compliance/regulatory** — A regulatory requirement not met by current tools.
- [ ] **Integration gap** — Data or process disconnect between existing tools.
- [ ] **Scale problem** — Current approach works at small scale but breaks under growth.
- [ ] **Cost problem** — Current solution is too expensive relative to value.
- [ ] **Accuracy/quality** — Current approach produces too many errors.
- [ ] **Speed/timing** — Current approach is too slow for business needs.
- [ ] **New opportunity** — Not a problem today, but an emerging need or trend.
