# Owner Solution Choice

> **Template ID:** `product/owner-solution-choice`
> **Phase 1N-B — Product Delivery Framework**
> **Optional lightweight template. Use when the Design Tree's Owner decision section is not enough.**

---

## Purpose

A lightweight decision record specifically for selecting the solution branch from the Design Tree before committing to a PRD. This template can stand alone or supplement the Design Tree's built-in Owner decision section.

---

## When to Use

Use this optional template when:

- The Design Tree's Owner decision section is insufficient.
- The decision needs to be communicated separately to stakeholders.
- The rationale for the selected branch is complex and deserves its own document.
- The team wants a standalone audit trail of the solution choice.
