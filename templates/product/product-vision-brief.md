## Purpose

A one-page founder-facing product vision that captures why this product matters, who it serves, and what it promises. This brief should be short enough to read in two minutes and clear enough to guide subsequent Research Discovery, Design Tree, and PRD work.

---

## When to Use

Use this brief when:

- A new product idea or line of work is proposed.
- An existing idea needs a crisp, communicable north star before committing to deeper research or design.
- The Owner wants a lightweight framing document before deciding whether to proceed into Research Discovery.

Do not use this brief as a substitute for a PRD. It is intentionally short and directional.
