# Rejected Options

> **Template ID:** `product/rejected-options`
> **Phase 1N-B — Product Delivery Framework**
> **Optional lightweight template. Use when the Design Tree's built-in rejected branches section is not enough.**

---

## Purpose

Explicitly record solution branches, technologies, architectures, or product directions that were considered and rejected. This prevents revisiting the same rejected option without new evidence and creates an audit trail of trade-off decisions.

---

## When to Use

Use this optional template when:

- The Design Tree's rejected branches section is insufficient.
- A significant technology choice was rejected and the rationale should survive longer than the Decision Tree.
- The team changes over time and decisions need to outlast individual memory.
- A rejected option is likely to be revisited by future stakeholders.
