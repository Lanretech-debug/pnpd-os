# Opportunity Scoring

Standardised scoring template for ranking, comparing, and prioritising product opportunities.

## 10 Scoring Dimensions

Each dimension scored **1 (weak) to 5 (strong)**. Total: 10-50 points.

| # | Dimension | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | Weight |
|---|-----------|---|---|---|---|---|---|--------|
| 1 | **Market Size** | <$10M TAM | $10-50M TAM | $50-200M TAM | $200M-1B TAM | $1B+ TAM | 2x |
| 2 | **Pain Intensity** | Nice-to-have | Slight efficiency gain | Clear workflow pain | Critical ops dependency | Can't run business without it | 3x |
| 3 | **Digitisation Gap** | Fully digitised | Mostly digital tools | Mix of digital + paper | Mostly paper/manual | Zero digitisation | 2x |
| 4 | **Founder-Market Fit** | No domain knowledge | Some awareness | Working knowledge | Domain practitioner | Industry insider/operator | 2x |
| 5 | **Moat Potential** | Commodity feature | Brand moat only | Data moat | Workflow lock-in | Data + workflow + compliance | 2x |
| 6 | **Acquisition Cost** | >$500 CAC | $200-500 CAC | $100-200 CAC | $50-100 CAC | <$50 CAC or viral | 1x |
| 7 | **Revenue Potential** | <$10 ARPU/mo | $10-30 ARPU/mo | $30-100 ARPU/mo | $100-500 ARPU/mo | $500+ ARPU/mo | 2x |
| 8 | **Expansion Revenue** | None | Seat upsells | Module upsells | Platform expansion | Embedded finance/app store | 1x |
| 9 | **Regulatory Tailwind** | Heavy regulatory drag | Compliance burden | Neutral | Mild tailwind | Strong regulatory push | 1x |
| 10 | **AI-Native Advantage** | No AI opportunity | AI as nice add-on | AI as differentiator | AI as core product | AI creates new category | 1x |

## Weighted Scoring Formula

```
Weighted Score = Sum (Dimension Score x Weight)
Max Possible = 170
```

## Scoring Bands

| Band | Weighted Score | Recommendation |
|------|----------------|----------------|
| Green | 120-170 | Pursue aggressively — strong opportunity |
| Yellow | 80-119 | Validate further — proceed with caution |
| Orange | 50-79 | Low priority — revisit if conditions change |
| Red | 10-49 | Avoid — fundamental gaps exist |
