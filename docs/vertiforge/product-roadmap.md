## Horizon Structure

Roadmaps are grouped into three horizons:

| Horizon | Timeframe | Goal | Confidence |
|---------|-----------|------|------------|
| **H1 — Now** | Current / next 4 weeks | Ship increment, validate hypothesis | High |
| **H2 — Next** | 4-12 weeks | Build validated features, expand | Medium |
| **H3 — Future** | 3-12 months | New markets, new business models, AI-native shifts | Low |

## Roadmap Principles

1. **Ship the smallest valuable increment** — each H1 item must deliver standalone value
2. **Evidence gates between horizons** — H2 items require validation from H1; H3 items require validation from H2
3. **No roadmap item survives contact with users** — adjust based on feedback
4. **One current milestone at a time** — focus reduces cycle risk
